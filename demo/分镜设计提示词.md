# 分镜设计提示词

## 通用风格后缀

```
dark Chinese mythology aesthetic, ink wash elements, cinematic atmosphere, cold blue-grey color palette, mist and cloud atmosphere, epic mythical feeling, 4K film still quality
```

---

## Panel 1.1 - WS (Wide Shot) - 悟空背影 + 白骨精浮现

```
Wide shot storyboard panel, low angle view. 

Scene: A lone figure (Sun Wukong) stands with back to camera in foreground, silhouette against misty mountain landscape. In the distance, a white ethereal figure (Baigujing) slowly emerges from swirling fog and clouds.

Composition: Small silhouette in foreground left, ethereal white figure emerging in distant center-right, vast atmospheric perspective.

Mood: Mysterious, ominous, tense anticipation.

Style: dark Chinese mythology aesthetic, ink wash elements, cinematic atmosphere, cold blue-grey color palette, mist and cloud atmosphere, epic mythical feeling, 4K film still quality
```

---

## Panel 1.2 - MS (Medium Shot) - 悟空转身，金箍棒抬起

```
Medium shot storyboard panel, eye level view.

Scene: Sun Wukong (anthropomorphic monkey with golden fur in dark ornate armor) slowly turning to face the camera. His golden staff (Ruyi Jingu Bang) is being raised diagonally across the frame. Fierce determined expression.

Composition: Character centered, staff leading the eye diagonally, mist and clouds in background.

Mood: Tense, determined, confrontation building.

Style: dark Chinese mythology aesthetic, ink wash elements, cinematic atmosphere, cold blue-grey color palette, mist and cloud atmosphere, epic mythical feeling, 4K film still quality
```

---

## Panel 1.3 - CU (Close-Up) - 眼神交汇

```
Close-up shot storyboard panel, slight low angle.

Scene: Intense close-up of Sun Wukong's face showing fierce golden-red glowing eyes (Fiery Golden Eyes), determined expression. In the reflection of his eyes, a faint white skeletal figure can be seen. Dramatic lighting.

Composition: Face fills frame, eyes are focal point, dramatic shadows.

Mood: Intense confrontation, unstoppable force meets immovable object.

Style: dark Chinese mythology aesthetic, ink wash elements, cinematic atmosphere, cold blue-grey color palette, mist and cloud atmosphere, epic mythical feeling, 4K film still quality
```

---

## Panel 1.4 - ECU (Extreme Close-Up) - 火眼金睛发光

```
Extreme close-up shot storyboard panel, eye level.

Scene: Extreme close-up of Sun Wukong's eye, specifically the Fiery Golden Eyes. The golden-red glow is intense, like flames burning within. Pupil is golden with red rim. Dramatic lighting creates strong contrast.

Composition: Single eye fills frame, glowing effect is central focus, no other elements.

Mood: Powerful, supernatural, the moment before action.

Style: dark Chinese mythology aesthetic, ink wash elements, cinematic atmosphere, cold blue-grey color palette, mist and cloud atmosphere, epic mythical feeling, 4K film still quality
```
